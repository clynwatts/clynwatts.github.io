I am a hydrologist and Assistant Professor at Binghamton University.  interested in the evolving relationships between water, land, and people. Broadly, my research includes: wetland hydrology, flood hazard mitigation, temperature as a groundwater tracer, drones as research tool. I am always seeking projects that are community-based and address a community need.
