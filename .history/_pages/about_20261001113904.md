I am a hydrologist and Assistant Professor at Binghamton University, interested in the evolving relationships between water, land, and people. Broadly, my research focuses on freshwater wetland restorations, with specific interest in restoring forested swamps, dam removals, and the role natural and restored wetlands play in flood hazard mitigation. Beyond wetlands, I am always seeking water related projects that are community-based and address a community need.
